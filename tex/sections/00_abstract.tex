The southern African Cheetah (\textit{Acinonyx jubatus jubatus}), has had their range decreased drastically in the past decades. I used a multivariate analysis in which the core variables were fixed at the historical baseline, so the temporal signal came from landscape change. This has only been exacerbated in the past decades due to both natural and anthropogenic reasons, leaving their current extant range majority outside protected areas. This leaves "barriers" like ranchers, roads and other human development in areas where cheetahs will be roaming.
