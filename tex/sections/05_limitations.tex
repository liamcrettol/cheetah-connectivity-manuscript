\paragraph{Fixed historical cores.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: the cores were created
from 2010--2016 evidence and stayed in the same places in every modeled year. The
analysis therefore measures changes in the surrounding landscape, not changes in the
actual locations of cheetah populations. This is the most important limitation.}

\paragraph{Structural, not functional.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: the model shows
where the landscape appears easier or harder to cross. It does not prove that cheetahs
used those places.}

\paragraph{Sampling bias in the occurrence baseline.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE:
some places may have fewer records simply because fewer people surveyed them. A place
with no record cannot automatically be treated as a place with no cheetahs.}

\paragraph{Resistance is expert-assigned.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: resistance
values were based on published evidence and stated assumptions rather than being
estimated from GPS movement data. Testing several assumptions shows whether conclusions
change, but it cannot prove that any one set of values matches real movement.}

\paragraph{Static covariates.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: roads, livestock, fences,
and terrain affected where resistance was high or low, but their maps did not change
between years. They shaped the geography of the result but could not cause modeled
change through time.}

\paragraph{Mapping completeness varies by country.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: some
countries have more complete road, fence, or other mapping than others. Apparent
differences may partly reflect differences in available data.}
% Sentence starter: "Differences in data coverage among countries may ..."

\paragraph{Geographic transferability.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: results and
resistance values should not be copied to another region without checking whether its
cheetahs and landscapes are comparable.}
% Sentence starter: "The results should not be transferred to other regions without ..."

\paragraph{Scope.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: the model does not include climate,
human-wildlife conflict, or whether cheetahs currently occupy each core. It cannot
answer questions that depend on those missing factors.}

\paragraph{Rainfall is not in the model.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: rainfall data
were not included. In dry areas, vegetation and bare ground can change simply because
one year is wetter or drier. Some modeled change may therefore reflect short-term
rainfall differences rather than permanent human-caused change.}

\paragraph{The vegetation term is narrower than it sounds.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE
GUIDE: mathematically, the vegetation score mainly responds to the percentage of bare
ground. Replacing tree cover with non-tree vegetation does not change the score if the
amount of bare ground stays the same. Describe this as a bare-ground indicator, not a
detailed model of vegetation structure.}

\paragraph{Only one core definition, one resolution.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE: the
analysis used one definition of a core and one 1-km cell size. The planned alternative
core definitions and 2-km and 5-km checks were not run. The study therefore cannot
show whether a different core rule or cell size would give the same answer.}

\paragraph{Current is reported outside the cores.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE:
Circuitscape treats cores as the places where current enters and leaves, so cores
automatically receive very high values. Removing core cells from the reported maps
makes the connecting landscape easier to examine, but the cores still influenced the
original calculation. Also report that the resistance grid contained 34 disconnected
pieces.}

\paragraph{Route location is less certain than route cost.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE
GUIDE: 49 of 180 route-and-year comparisons had less than 80 percent overlap within
1 km, including 21 of the 32 priority links. The model can consistently show that two
cores are relatively easy to connect while still being uncertain about the exact path
between them.}

\paragraph{Input series are not perfectly comparable.} \TODO{PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE:
the nighttime-light product changes version in 2024, and the 2012 built-up layer is
represented by data from 2010. These mismatches may create small artificial
differences between years. Nighttime lights received the smallest weight, which limits
but does not remove this concern.}
