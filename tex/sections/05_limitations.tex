\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The model shows where movement may be easier or harder, but it does not show where cheetahs actually traveled. The cores and several landscape layers were held constant through time, which allowed a fair comparison between years but limited what could change. The resistance values were set by the author, guided by published studies, and were not fitted to cheetah movement data; rainfall was not checked, so some vegetation change may reflect wet or dry years rather than land use, and source years did not always match the snapshot years. The mapped paths are broad planning areas rather than exact travel routes, and the only independent check came from nine animals in one district before 2012, which could not test the fence component and found a lean carried mostly by roads. Longer-running tracking datasets exist for other parts of the network, such as the Okavango Delta and central Namibia, but they are held privately and were not requested within this project, so the results should be tested further before use at another scale or in another region.
