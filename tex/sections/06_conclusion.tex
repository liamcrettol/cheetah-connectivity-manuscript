\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Modeled connectivity between fixed southern African cheetah range cores changed only slightly between 2012 and 2024, with individual links changing in both directions rather than declining together. The strongest concentrations of modeled current often reached protected areas, but the wider connecting landscape was less protected than the study area as a whole. Thirty-two links were retained as main priorities, and 13 were identified as places where more field information is needed. These are modeled landscape connections, not observed cheetah routes, and they are best used to decide where field monitoring should begin.
